Drop square student headshots here (jpg or png). File-name convention:
  <lastname>.jpg   e.g.  rifa.jpg, islam.jpg, thapa-magar.jpg

Then in data/students.js set the student's `photo` field to
  photo: "assets/img/students/<lastname>.jpg"

Only current students (phd/ms/bs/postdoc) support photos; alumni cards
stay text-only. If a file is missing, the card falls back to the
student's initials on a navy circle.
